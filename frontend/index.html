How it works

From upload to spreadsheet in four steps.

Upload

Drop one or many files, or start from a sample document.

Read

Text is pulled straight from PDFs and Word files. Scans and photos go through OCR.

Extract and verify

The AI structures the text. Docnexa keeps only values found in the document and runs its checks.

Review and export

Fix or approve fields, ask questions, then download Excel, JSON or CSV. Everything is saved to History.
